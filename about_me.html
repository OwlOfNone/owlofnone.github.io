About Me

I'm Lisa, also known as Owl of None or Oon, an artist and creative jack-of-all-trades from southern Germany.

In my art, I focus on contemporary themes, using all the materials available to me.

Because of my love for character design, world-building, and fantasy, portraits also play a central role in my art.

I don't just rely on traditional media, I also use digital media to create art in the form of images and 3D models.

Before turning to art, I studied neurobiology. My interest in nature and how our brains work frequently finds its way into my art.
