Commissions

You can commission digital artworks from me, either for yourself or as a gift.

Please note the following points before submitting your request:

  • I only offer digital artwork commissions, which means you will receive the finished picture as a file via email only. Of course, you may print out the completed commissioned work yourself at any time, at your own expense.

  • By requesting a commission, you agree to the terms and conditions for commissions listed below. Therefore, please read these terms carefully before submitting your request.

  • Please describe the picture that you want as precisely as possible and provide reference images.

  • The amount of work involved — and thus the price for your commission — depends on many factors, such as the number of people or objects depicted, the complexity of the perspective and composition, and the level of detail of the background.

  • Please let me know in your request if you do not want me to publish the images created as part of the commission.

2. Electronic Format

You can only commission digital images. Once the commissioned work is complete and payment has been confirmed, you will receive the finished work as a image file via email.

It is expressly not possible to request, order shipment of, or pick up physical pictures.

I reserve the right to discontinue work on a commission at any time and without providing a reason. If you have already made payments for the canceled commission, you will, of course, receive a refund.
